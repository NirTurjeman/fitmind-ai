The vision

Less dashboard. More understanding.

Wearables capture useful information, but understanding trends often means digging through charts. FitMind AI aims to make personal data exploration conversational, transparent, and accessible.

☾

Sleep patterns

Ask about sleep duration and trends across weeks or months, with clear comparisons grounded in available records.

♡

Heart & recovery trends

Explore resting heart rate, HRV, and activity patterns when supported by the wearable and authorized data source.

✧

Ask in your own words

Use natural-language questions instead of navigating complex dashboards. See the data behind each insight.

Proposed experience

Three simple steps.

1

Connect a wearable

Authorize access to supported Fitbit data using the provider's official permissions and consent flow.

2

Connect an AI experience

Our goal is to support eligible ChatGPT account-based access, subject to OpenAI approval and applicable product terms.

3

Explore your data

Ask questions, compare periods, and understand trends through explainable answers grounded in your own records.

Responsible by design

Privacy and trust come first.

Project status: FitMind AI is currently a product concept. Wearable connectivity, ChatGPT integration, and the mobile app have not yet been implemented or approved. Our intended design prioritizes explicit user consent, limited data access, secure handling, and controls for deletion and data sharing. We will publish a complete privacy policy before collecting personal health data.

Health disclaimer: FitMind AI is intended for informational wellness insights, not medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency monitoring. Correlation does not establish causation.

Independence: FitMind AI is an independent proposed product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by Google, Fitbit, or OpenAI.
